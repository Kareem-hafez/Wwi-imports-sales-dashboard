## What I would do next

**Review the Halloween zombie masks**They sell well ($130K–$162K per size) but lose about 6% on every sale. The four sizes lost about $33K together. Check whether this was a clearance discount or a pricing error.

**Reprice or renegotiate the air cushion machine**It is the top seller but has the lowest margin of the top five (40%). A small price rise here moves total profit more than anywhere else.

**Learn from the 20 mm double-sided wrap**It earns 85% margin, while the 10 mm and 32 mm versions of the same product earn 47%. Find out whether price or cost explains the gap.

Source: FactSale and DimStockItem tables, cleaned in Excel. Losses are small overall ($40.5K against $9.9M profit) but concentrated, which is why they are easy to fix. Margin = profit ÷ sales excluding tax. This page was built as a design example of a product-focused view.
