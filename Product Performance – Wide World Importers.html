What I would do next

Review the Halloween zombie masksThey sell well ($130K–$162K per size) but lose about 6% on every sale. The four sizes lost about $33K together. Check whether this was a clearance discount or a pricing error.
Reprice or renegotiate the air cushion machineIt is the top seller but has the lowest margin of the top five (40%). A small price rise here moves total profit more than anywhere else.
Learn from the 20 mm double-sided wrapIt earns 85% margin, while the 10 mm and 32 mm versions of the same product earn 47%. Find out whether price or cost explains the gap.
